# Calibration log — Children's Wellbeing Act, attendance, workforce, early years

## Children's Wellbeing & Schools Act 2026 (c.21, Royal Assent 29 Apr 2026)
- **Breakfast clubs (s.30):** statutory free primary breakfast; early adopters Apr 2025 (750 schools), national
  rollout Apr 2026. Funding £25/day + £1/pupil/day; £1,000 start-up; £80m FY26-27. DfE take-up assumption 50%.
  BCR 2.43 (y1). Effects: **+2 months KS1 attainment (moderate-LOW security; KS2 NULL after re-analysis)**;
  **attendance ~26 fewer half-days/yr per class of 30 ≈ −0.43 days/pupil/yr**.
- **FSM expansion (s.32):** all UC households from **Sept 2026**. +500k immediate → ~1.7m long-run (3.39m UC
  children Nov 2024; 7-in-10 not currently FSM-eligible). −100k children out of relative poverty (DfE, assumes
  100% take-up — discount). Cost ~£250m/yr → ~£1bn/yr long-run. Meal cost £2.61. Pupil Premium trigger.
  Attainment (universal-primary pilot, OPTIMISTIC CEILING): +2mo/2yr for newly-eligible only.
- **Uniform cap (s.35):** ≤3 branded items, ~Sept 2026. Saving £14 primary / £19 secondary per pupil. 4.2m pupils.
- **QTS for academy teachers (s.53): 1 Sept 2027.** Non-QTS stock 14,485 FTE (3.1%); special/AP 9.2%.
- Other: national pay floor + national curriculum to academies; end academy presumption; Children Not in School
  registers (ss.37-42, NOT yet commenced).

## Attendance (the central mediating hub)
- Overall absence **6.63%** (2024/25 aut+spr), pre-pandemic 4.7%. **Persistent absence 17.63% (~1.29m)**, pre-pandemic
  10.53%. Severe absence 2.26% (~166k), STILL RISING (model as separate sticky state).
- FSM persistent absence **29.9% vs 12.7%** non-FSM (2.4×); FSM severe 4.2% vs 1.2% (3.6×).
- EHCP persistent absence 34.1%, SEN support 30.1%, no-SEN 19.4%.
- **Dose-response (DfE Mar 2025, covariate-adjusted): KS4 95-100% attendance → 1.9× odds of grade5+ E&M vs 90-95%;
  ~10 days absence halves odds. KS2 1.3×. Each 5% band ≈ ×1.3-1.9 odds.** (Observational, not RCT.)
- Attendance mentors: 59% of severely absent / 45% of persistently absent improve (UNCONTROLLED — haircut 30-50%).
  £15m/3yr pilot, >10,000 pupils. Attendance Hubs (32, ~2,000 schools). RISE attendance/behaviour hubs.

## Post-16 / skills / NEET (the exit boundary)
- **NEET 16-24: 13.3% (~840k, late 2025)**; 18-24 NEET 16.0%; increasingly INACTIVITY/mental-health driven
  (add exogenous youth ill-health term). Unqualified ~2× NEET vs GCSE-qualified.
- Skills England (2024); Growth & Skills Levy; Foundation apprenticeships (£2,000); L7 defunding; **Post-16 White
  Paper 20 Oct 2025** → V Levels, BTEC defunding paused/delayed; T Levels 25,508 starts (2024). Apprenticeship
  starts 353,500 (u19 share down to 21.2%). Youth Guarantee + Youth Jobs Grant (£3,000). LLE Sept 2026.
- **Tuition fee cap £9,535 (2025/26)** (from £9,250). 16-19 funding +£450m real 2025→26/27.

## Teacher workforce & funding (route outcomes through teacher QUALITY, NOT £/pupil)
- ~468,000 FTE teachers (Nov 2024). 6,500 pledge = net stock secondary+special+FE by 2029, baseline 284,626
  (primary excluded; retention counts). Leaving 9.6%/yr; **~32% gone within 5yr**. Vacancy 6/1000 (2× pre-pandemic;
  up to 46% in most-deprived inner London).
- ITT secondary 62% of target (2024/25) → ~89% (2025/26); physics 31%→~93%, maths now over-target; MFL/music/D&T/
  business chronically short. **Bursary +£5,000 ⇒ ~+15% trainees** (strongest workforce lever).
- Per-pupil real funding: secondary ~£7,400, primary ~£6,700 (2024/25); aggregate back above 2010 but mainstream
  squeezed by SEND (+50% real since 2019/20), NI (£800m), pay. 2025 SR ≈ per-pupil real FREEZE (~0.4%/yr).
  Starting salary £31,650; experienced −9% real vs 2010.
- **Funding→attainment elasticity ≈ 0 / contested** (route via inputs). **Teacher quality→attainment STRONG:
  1 SD value-added ⇒ +0.10-0.20 SD pupil score/yr.** Class-size −1 to −2 months, very costly.

## Early years (the gap-origin & lag spine)
- **GLD age-5: 68.3% (2024/25); FSM gap 21.3pp (FSM 51.3% vs 72.5%). Target 75% by 2028 (Best Start, Jul 2025,
  £1.5bn/3yr; 1,000 Family Hubs).**
- EPI gap: age5 4.6-4.7mo, age11 10.0-10.3mo, **age16 19.1-19.2mo**. ~40% of age-16 gap present by age 5;
  ~60% of age-11 gap by age 7. age16 ≈ 4.2× age5.
- **Childcare expansion = WORKING-PARENT subsidy (Mar 2023): 30h all 9mo-school-age from Sept 2025.** Spend
  £8.7bn (2025-26), doubled from £4.4bn. Rates: under-2 £11.54, 2yo £8.53, 3-4yo £6.12/hr. Take-up ~530k.
  **CRITICAL: NOT gap-closing — only ~20% of bottom-third earners eligible vs ~80% of >£45k. Funded hours buy
  EEF "+3mo quantity" effect, NOT "+5 to +7mo quality" effects. Apply 0.5-0.7× disadvantaged-coverage multiplier.**
- **EYPP £1.00/hr (2025-26) → £1.15/hr (2026-27) ≈ £570-655/yr** (was ~¼ of pupil premium).
- EEF effect sizes (months/yr): comms&language +7, parental engagement +5, early literacy +4, extra hours +3.
- **11-yr lag EY→GCSE; ~6-yr EY→KS2. Fade-out 30-60% by age 16 unless reinforced. US ROI haircut 0.4-0.7×.**
- Heckman birth-to-5 ROI ~13%/yr (intensive US RCTs — haircut). Provider exit: childminders −5%/yr.
