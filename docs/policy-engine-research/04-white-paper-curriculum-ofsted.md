# Calibration log — Schools White Paper, Francis Review, Ofsted, RISE

## Policies
- **Schools White Paper "Every Child Achieving and Thriving" (CP 1508-I, Feb 2026).** Two headline ambitions:
  (1) national average **Attainment 8 → 50** ("grade 5 across GCSEs on average"); (2) **halve the KS4 disadvantage
  gap** (15.5 → **7.7** points; >1.3m grade improvements; >30,000 more disadvantaged passing E&M/yr).
  Delivery: 6,500 expert teachers; Inclusive Mainstream Fund £1.6bn/3yr; Experts at Hand £1.8bn; £200m SEND
  training; enrichment entitlement; all schools in a "high-quality trust"; Mission North East + Mission Coastal;
  Reading Ambition / Unlocking Reading + statutory **Year 8 reading test**; Year-1 phonics 80%→**90%**; GLD→75%.
- **Curriculum & Assessment Review (Francis):** interim Mar 2025, final 5 Nov 2025 ("Building a world-class
  curriculum for all"). **EBacc abolished** as a measure; **Progress 8 reformed** (new 8-slot breadth model, GCSEs
  from Sept 2027 — govt diverged from review which wanted P8 kept); **−10% KS4 exam time**; oracy; Year-8
  diagnostics; future-facing computing; citizenship mandatory in primary; new curriculum first teaching **Sept 2028**.
- **Ofsted:** single-word judgements abolished → **report cards**, 5-point scale (Urgent improvement→Exceptional),
  6 evaluation areas + separate safeguarding, "secure fit", deep dives removed. Live **10 Nov 2025**. JR refused.
- **RISE (Regional Improvement for Standards & Excellence):** replaces forced academisation (CWSA repeals the duty
  to issue academy orders). £20m, 65 advisers, support 12-24mo. **639 "stuck" schools, ~292,000 pupils** (Jun 2025).

## Baselines (KS4 / KS2) — use Attainment 8 (Progress 8 unavailable 2024/25-25/26, no COVID KS2 baseline)
- **Attainment 8 all: 46.0 (2024/25)** (was 46.7 in 2018/19). **A8 disadvantage gap: 15.5 points** (13.6 in 2018/19;
  widening is almost entirely a disadvantaged-group DECLINE: dis −1.8, non-dis +0.1 since 2019).
  Derived: disadvantaged A8 ≈ **36.7**, non-disadvantaged ≈ **52.2**.
- **Grade 5+ English&Maths: ~45.5% all** (approx; 45% firm for 2022/23); **disadvantaged ~25.8%, others ~53.1%,
  gap 27.3pp** (held). EBacc APS 4.09; EBacc entry 40.5%.
- **KS2 RWM expected standard: 62% all / 47% disadvantaged / 69% others (2024/25)** [WP cites 60.7%/45.5% for
  2023/24]. Reading 75% (peak), writing 72%, maths 74%. Phonics Y1 ~80%. Targets: RWM > 64.9%/51.3% (above 2019).
- **EPI months gap: KS4 19.1, KS2 10.0, age-5 4.7 (2024); persistent 22.4mo KS4.** Secondary widening since 2019
  "entirely explained by higher absence among disadvantaged pupils." Regional: London 10.2mo vs West Midlands 19.2mo.
- **Absence target: 7.15% (23/24) → 5.85% by 2028/29** (−1.3pp), +20m days attended.

## Effect sizes (mostly ASSUMPTION — recent, un-evaluated; treat as wide-prior sensitivity)
- EBacc removal / P8 breadth reform: ~near-zero direct attainment effect (composition change).
- Curriculum refresh (2028): unknown, plausibly ±0-2 A8 over a decade.
- Ofsted report cards: workload AMBIGUOUS (deep dives cut ↓ vs secure-fit/6 areas ↑); standards effect unknown.
  Model two scenarios (retention up vs workload up).
- RISE targeted intervention: small-moderate for worst schools (analogy London Challenge / sponsored academies
  ~0.1-0.3 SD, mixed); highly uncertain at scale; only ~4% of pupils.
- End of forced academisation: ~near-zero net attainment (academisation premium was mixed/null).
- Reading CPD: EEF literacy/oral-language analogues +2 to +5 months.
- **Attendance recovery = highest-leverage gap channel (EPI mechanism, sourced).**
- Inclusive Mainstream + Experts at Hand: uncertain; WP cites mainstream-vs-special ~+½ grade E&M (selection bias).
- **A8→50 + gap→7.7 are TARGETS not forecasts — unprecedented pace on flat mainstream funding; treat as stretch.**

## Funding context (IFS)
Real per-pupil ≈ 2010 level; **mainstream +5% only since 2019-20** (SEND absorbed >half the rise; mainstream ≈
2015-16 level). SEND→**£6bn LA high-needs gap by 2028-29** (OBR). Simultaneity of reforms (2025-2029) makes single
-reform attribution near-impossible — model the BUNDLE.
