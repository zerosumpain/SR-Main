# Calibration log — Milburn / social mobility / disadvantage gap

## Identification
"Milburn paper" = **Young People and Work: Interim Report** (28 May 2026), Alan Milburn lead reviewer
for DWP. Diagnosis: youth economic inactivity driven by **mental ill-health/neurodiversity** ("generational
fault line"). Frames welfare→"Working State". Final report due late 2026. It is the **NEET/exit boundary**
of an education model, not a schools lever. Historic context: *Unleashing Aspiration* (2009 fair access);
chair of Social Mobility Commission 2012–17. SMC *State of the Nation 2025* (Dec 2025): UK mid-pack,
declining upward mobility, severe regional cold spots.

## Sourced baselines (use directly)
- **Disadvantage attainment gap (EPI 2025 revised "months" series — DO NOT mix with old 10.4mo series):**
  - Reception (age 5): **4.7 months** (2024 data)
  - KS2 (age 11): **10.0 months** (persistent-disadvantage 11.0)
  - **KS4 (age 16): 19.1 months** (persistent-disadvantage 22.4); +1.0mo since 2019 ⇒ **~+0.2 mo/yr drift**
  - 16–19: 3.3 grades (different metric)
- **Plain GCSE gap:** ~44% FSM achieve grade 4+ Eng&Maths vs >70% non-FSM.
- **Pupil Premium 2025-26:** primary **£1,515**, secondary **£1,075**, LAC £2,630; total **~£3.0bn / 2.3m pupils**
  ⇒ ~£1,300 avg. YoY uplift ~2.4%, historically below inflation.
- **Child poverty (2024/25):** relative AHC **31% ≈ 4.5m**; absolute AHC 27% (4.0m). Strategy target: lift
  **550,000** out of relative poverty by 2028/29 (two-child-limit removal + FSM expansion).
- **NEET 16–24 (Jan–Mar 2026): 13.5% (1.012m)** — highest in ~12 yrs; projected **1.25m** in 5y w/o reform.
  57% inactive / 43% unemployed. Disabled NEETs citing mental health ~doubled to >40%.
- **Funding equity (IFS):** spend on disadvantaged children ~halved in decade to 2024; EY deprivation boost
  60%(2017)→38%(2023); EY funding/hour 22% below 2017 peak; core schools budget ~flat real 2025-29.

## Critical modelling guidance (skeptical flags)
1. **No robust £/pupil → gap elasticity exists.** PP effectiveness evidence (EEF, Gorard 2022) is about
   spending *quality* (tiered model), not quantity. Model PP as a **quality-moderated offset** to drift with
   wide uncertainty, NOT a clean £→months coefficient.
2. **Absence is EPI's stated driver** of the entire post-2019 widening. Model income → **attendance** → gap
   as the mediated path; attendance is the highest-confidence lever.
3. **FSM→all-UC (Sept 2026) is a structural break** in the "disadvantaged" denominator — changes measured gap.
4. **Place-based programmes (Opportunity Areas/EIAs) discontinued** (March 2025) with weak measured effects.
5. Breakfast-club/EEF "+2 months" figure widely cited but verify; treat gov "behaviour/attendance/attainment"
   as directional.
