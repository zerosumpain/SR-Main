# Art attribution

Every licence for the art the Hometowns page and app ship is recorded here (spec §9.15, §13.2
WP8, §17). Nothing here comes from a GPL project.

| Art | Source | Licence |

The portraits are plain shapes from our own palette, written for this game. No reference
image was traced.

## Third-party packs

None yet. The planned art pass (M3) recolours Kenney's isometric city, road and landscape packs,
which are CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication; credit given voluntarily). When they are added,
list each pack here with its version and download URL, and credit Kenney (kenney.nl) on the app's
About screen.
