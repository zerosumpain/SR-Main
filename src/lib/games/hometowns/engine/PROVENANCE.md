# Provenance

Mechanics inspired by public descriptions of 1989-era city simulators and by academic urban
economics; no GPL code, art or text was used.

This engine was written clean-room from the Hometowns design specification and its research
notes (behavioural descriptions only). Its structure, names, data types, constants and tables are
our own: aggregate cohort stocks, a logit migration choice, sector margins and district gravity,
tuned by our own balance Lab. No code from any other game, open-source or private, was copied or
paraphrased into it.
