# jkai-research

Clarify the question, decision and evidence needed. Discover current research
and search tools using `tool_search`, then read their schemas with
`tool_describe`. Use the current supported capabilities rather than copying
node types from old workflow examples.

Prefer primary sources. Read the relevant passages, compare dates and claims,
and keep source links beside the facts they support. Separate source evidence
from your inference and identify unresolved contradictions. Never invent a
citation or imply that an unread document was checked.

Use `knowledge_search` for saved evidence where relevant. A failed retrieval
does not establish that a claim is false. Report the access or coverage limit.
Return the requested answer with its supporting evidence, not an unbounded
research process.
