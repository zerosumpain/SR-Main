How to play — Brass & Rails

a quick guide
🎯
Goal

Out-build and out-fight rival houses. Win by Domination (take every capital), the Great Exhibition (all tech + deep science), a Railway Empire (huge combined city levels), or top score at turn 70.

⌖
Get around

Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, and pan with W A S D or the arrow keys. The ⌖ button recentres on your capital.

♟
Units

Click your unit to select it. Blue tiles = where it can march; red = enemies it can strike. The panel shows exactly what that unit can do — found, scout, attack or move.

⌂
Cities

Click a city to build units with Production ⚒ and build infrastructure nearby — Farms, Lumber Mills, Ports, Water Mills, Mines. Each improvement unlocks one city level (hamlet → skyscrapers at Lv 10).

⚒
Economy

Tiles yield: fields/rivers → food, forest → wood, hills/peaks → stone & science. Research the matching tech, then build improvements to grow your cities and bank Science 🔬 for the tech tree.

🌫
Fog & the AI

You only see what your units & cities can — scout to reveal the map. The AI plays under the same fog, and it learns from every defeat (see the 🧠 dossier) and adapts to beat you.
