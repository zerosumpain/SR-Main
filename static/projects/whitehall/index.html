How to play — Whitehall

a quick guide
🎯
Goal

Out-build and out-manoeuvre rival departments. Win by Takeover (control every rival's lead department), a Royal Commission (all reforms + deep research), the Machinery of Government (huge combined departmental levels), or top score at turn 70.

⌖
Get around

Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, and pan with W A S D or the arrow keys. The ⌖ button recentres on your lead department.

♟
Roles

Click your role to select it. Blue tiles = where it can march; red = rivals it can challenge. The panel shows exactly what that role can do — establish, survey, contest or move.

⌂
Departments

Click a department to recruit roles with Taxes 💷 and build infrastructure nearby — Recruitment Drives, Estates Offices, Overseas Posts, Shared Service Centres, Procurement Hubs. Each improvement unlocks one departmental level (sub-office → flagship department at Lv 10).

💷
Economy

Tiles yield: fields/rivers → Staff, forest → MP Favours, hills/peaks → Red Tape & Policy Points. Enact the matching reform, then build improvements to grow your departments and bank Policy Points 📜 for the Reform Programme.

🌫
Fog & the AI

You only see what your roles & departments can — survey to reveal the map. The AI plays under the same fog, and it learns from every defeat and adapts to beat you.
