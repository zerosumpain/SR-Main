A school's budget is not one payment. It is the sum of several streams, each with its own rule, and the formula that combines them is the whole of the answer to "why does this school get more than that one?".

What changes when the roll changes

Most of the money follows pupils. Move the roll and watch the total: the per-pupil entitlement is the largest single term in the formula.
