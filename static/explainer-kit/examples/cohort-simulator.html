Who is missed when an entitlement still depends on an application?

Fact Parliamentary and Education Policy Institute analysis estimates that about 11% of eligible children in England—roughly 215,000—were not registered for free school meals. Registration also affects the pupil-premium count used for school funding.

Automatic registration changes the direction of the burden: the system identifies eligibility and families opt out, rather than families proving eligibility before a child is counted. The uncertain part is how many of those currently missed an operational scheme would actually reach.
